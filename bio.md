I did my B.Sc. (Honours) in Physics at Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi (2018–2021), and joined ICTS-TIFR in 2021 for my integrated PhD. In 2026 I was a Visiting PhD Fellow at Nordita, Stockholm, and I have visited research groups in Hamburg, Bonn, Stockholm and Helsinki.

Outside research, I teach and take part in public outreach at ICTS.
